Questions

Which breathing exercise is best for anxiety?

For quick relief, try the physiological sigh: two inhales through the nose, then one long exhale. In a 2023 Stanford study, five minutes of daily cyclic sighing improved mood and lowered breathing rate more than mindfulness meditation. For a longer practice, box breathing and coherent breathing both steady the nervous system.

Which breathing exercise helps you fall asleep?

4-7-8 breathing is the usual choice for sleep: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8. The long exhale slows the heart rate. Try it lying down with the Midnight mood light for a few minutes before bed.

How do you do box breathing?

Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4, exhale for 4, and hold again for 4. That is one square; repeat it for 3 to 5 minutes. Box breathing (Sama Vritti) is used by athletes and Navy SEALs to stay clear-headed under pressure.

What is coherent breathing?

Coherent, or resonance, breathing means breathing at about five breaths a minute: inhale for 5.5 seconds and exhale for 5.5 seconds. This pace is associated with higher heart rate variability and a calmer mood.

How long should I practise breathing exercises?

Three to ten minutes a day is enough for most people. A single physiological sigh can help in the moment; regular short sessions build the effect over time. nisvasa offers 3, 5 and 10 minute sessions or a custom length up to 60 minutes.

Is Wim Hof breathing safe?

Wim Hof breathing is safe for most healthy adults when practised seated or lying down. Never do it in or near water, while driving or standing, and skip it if you are pregnant or have a heart, blood-pressure or seizure condition. Tingling and light-headedness are common; stop if you feel unwell.
